RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of November 1, 2025, by and between Horizon Property Management LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Miller ("Tenant") for the premises located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Apt 4B, Springfield ("Premises").

1. Term of Lease
The initial lease term shall begin on December 1, 2025, and expire on November 30, 2026. This Lease shall automatically renew for successive two (2) year terms unless Tenant provides written cancellation notice by certified mail at least one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the expiration date. Upon automatic renewal, monthly rent shall automatically increase by twenty-five percent (25%).

2. Rent and Late Fees
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly rent of $2,400, due on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month. If rent is not received by 11:59 PM on the 2nd day of the month, Tenant shall pay an immediate late penalty fee of $250 plus an additional daily assessment of $35 for each day rent remains unpaid.

3. Security Deposit
Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $4,800 (equal to two months' rent) as security for faithful performance. Landlord shall hold the deposit in a non-interest-bearing account. A mandatory non-refundable administrative and turnover fee of $800 shall be deducted from the deposit regardless of premises condition upon move-out. Landlord shall have ninety (90) days following move-out to return any remaining balance.

4. Maintenance and Repairs
Tenant is solely responsible for all interior and exterior maintenance, repairs, and upkeep of the Premises, including plumbing blockages, electrical fixtures, heating and cooling system servicing, and appliance repairs, up to $1,500 per incident. Landlord shall only be responsible for foundational structural repairs exceeding $5,000, provided Tenant notifies Landlord in writing via certified mail within forty-eight (48) hours of occurrence.

5. Landlord Right of Entry
Landlord, its agents, contractors, and prospective buyers or tenants reserve the unrestricted right to enter the Premises at any hour of the day or night without prior notice for inspections, maintenance, appraisal, or showing. Tenant waives any statutory requirement for advance notice or quiet enjoyment during such entries.

6. Alterations and Improvements
Tenant shall not drive nails into walls, paint, install window coverings, or make any structural or decorative alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written permission. Any unauthorized alterations, including wall hangings, will result in an immediate forfeiture of the security deposit.

7. Subletting and Assignment
Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or allow any guest or visitor to occupy the Premises for more than forty-eight (48) consecutive hours without Landlord's written consent and payment of a $500 non-refundable guest registration fee. Any unauthorized guest will be considered an unauthorized sublease subject to immediate eviction.

8. Rules and Regulations
Landlord reserves the right to adopt and amend community rules and regulations at any time. Notice posted on the building bulletin board shall constitute immediate and binding effect upon Tenant. Violation of any community rule shall incur a $150 fine per violation.

9. Default and Acceleration of Rent
If Tenant fails to pay rent when due or breaches any covenant herein, Landlord may terminate this Lease upon twenty-four (24) hours' notice. Upon such termination, all remaining rent due for the unexpired term of the Lease shall immediately become due and payable as accelerated liquidated damages.

10. Indemnification and Liability Waiver
Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord from any and all liability, claims, or damages for bodily injury, property loss, or theft occurring on or about the Premises, regardless of whether caused by Landlord's failure to maintain common areas or Landlord's own negligence. Tenant agrees to hold Landlord harmless for any mold, water damage, or pest infestations.

11. Quiet Enjoyment and As-Is Acceptance
Tenant accepts the Premises in strict "AS-IS, WHERE-IS" condition with all latent and patent defects. Landlord makes no representations or warranties concerning habitability, energy efficiency, or environmental safety.

12. Utilities and Services
Tenant shall be solely responsible for arranging and paying all utilities and municipal fees associated with the Premises, including water, sewer, trash collection, electricity, gas, and a proportional share of common area utility expenses as billed by Landlord.

13. Governing Law and Venue
This Lease shall be governed exclusively by the laws of the county where the property is located. Tenant waives any right to trial by jury in any summary eviction or debt collection proceeding initiated by Landlord.

14. Entire Agreement
This Lease constitutes the sole and entire agreement between the parties. No oral representations or promises shall be binding unless reduced to writing and signed by Landlord.
